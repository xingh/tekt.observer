# Interface

```yaml
INTERFACE:
  character: calm, keyboard-friendly, document-centered
  visual_system:
    palette: restrained neutrals with one warm accent
    layout: generous whitespace, readable measure, subtle borders
    themes: accessible light and dark
    prohibition: do not copy Anthropic branding or assets
  navigation:
    primary: [workspace, inbox, watchers, digests]
    secondary: [operations, members, import-export, settings]
  essential_flows:
    onboarding: immediately show realistic topic_watch, job_watch, and market_watch content
    inbox: filter, inspect, score, curate, and use the keyboard throughout
    item: content, provenance, ranking detail, feedback, and source/run context
    operations: realtime queued/running/retrying/cancelled/completed progress
    collaboration: owner manages members; role limitations are visible before action
    exchange: export status, stale-revision refusal, immutable publish status, validated import/fork
  accessibility:
    requirements: [semantic HTML, visible focus, full keyboard operation, WCAG contrast, reduced-motion support]
```
